## 📌 Project Overview
Developed at **DAV Public School, Airoli** by **Dev Bajaj** and **Ishani Majumdar**, with field research support from **Godrej & Boyce Mangroves** (Dr. Hemant Karkhanis) and the **Thane Creek Flamingo Sanctuary**. 

This project engineers a **Wetland Plant Microbial Fuel Cell (PMFC / WMC)** and conducts a multi-stage experimental investigation into coastal mangrove ecosystems across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, targeting UN Sustainable Development Goals **SDG 7, 9, 11, 13, 14, and 15**.

---

## ⚡ Core Engineering & Experimental Modules

### 1. Wetland Plant Microbial Fuel Cell (WMC) — Clean Energy Generation
* **Mechanism:** Leverages plant-soil-microbe electrochemical synergy. As mangrove roots release organic compounds into anaerobic wetland soil, indigenous microbes participate in redox reactions and release electrons.
* **Hardware Setup:** An anode immersed in the mangrove soil captures released electrons and establishes a measurable electrical potential difference across a cathode, verified via galvanometer deflection.
* **Application:** Demonstrates zero-emission, decentralized off-grid bio-electricity generation for environmentally sensitive coastal regions.

### 2. Blue Carbon Sequestration & Microscopic Soil Analysis
* Conducted microscopic structural comparisons between anaerobic coastal wetland soils and normal terrestrial soils.
* Demonstrated chemical carbon capture mechanisms ($\text{NaHCO}_3$, $\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$, $\text{NaOH}$) modeling how tidal wetlands sequester **81 to 216 metric tons of Blue Carbon per acre**.

### 3. Bio-Filtration ("Kidneys of Landscapes")
* Engineered a wetland soil filtration column demonstrating natural removal of excess nitrogen, phosphorus, heavy metals, and harmful microbes to prevent coastal algal blooms and produce clear filtrate.

### 4. Coastal Flood Mitigation & Soil Stabilization
* Investigated red mangrove dense root-soil binding mechanics and tidal wetland salinity thresholds for sustainable coastal agriculture (paddy cultivation) and urban flood defense in Mumbai/Navi Mumbai.
